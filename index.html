04 research

A Survey of Hybrid Inference Systems for Large Language Models

TMLR, 2026 · Co-author

A survey of hybrid LLM inference spanning speculative decoding, reasoning, model routing, and multi-agent, hardware-aware inference. Introduces a four-level taxonomy — micro, macro, structural, and physical orchestration — and identifies the "Orchestration Gap": the lack of a unified control layer to balance inference latency against reasoning quality.
